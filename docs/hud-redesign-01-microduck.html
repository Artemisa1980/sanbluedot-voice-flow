REDESIGN 01 sanbluedot

What this is: the floating capsule rebuilt around the Microduck from microduck-mascot-01.html. The duck replaces the microphone disc and stands outside the capsule; the capsule keeps today's three rows — status and wordmark, the twelve-bar meter, the telemetry strip — and today's colours, badges and texts.

Three ways to join them: beside it, talking into it, or perched on top. Every panel runs the same simulated session, so the duck and the capsule change together: it listens and reacts to the voice while recording, looks around while processing, nods on delivery and shakes its head on an error or a cancel.

STATE ZOOM WALL

1 · THREE WAYS TO JOIN THE DUCK AND THE CAPSULE

A · BESIDE354 × 100 PANEL

The smallest change. The 44pt microphone disc and its 14pt gap leave the capsule; the duck stands to its left on the same baseline. The capsule shrinks from 330 to 254 wide.

The three rows keep their sizes, so the 12-character application cap and every build check on HUDLayout stay valid.

B · SPEECH BUBBLE344 × 100 PANEL

The capsule is what the duck hears. A small tail points from the capsule to the duck's bill, so the two read as one character and its speech.

Same rows and sizes as A. The tail is the only new shape; it sits at the head's height and stays put while the head moves.

C · PERCH300 × 132 PANEL

The duck sits on the capsule. The capsule drops to two rows: status, a compact meter and the wordmark on one line, telemetry under it. The duck stands on the top-left edge.

Narrowest footprint across the screen, but the tallest: it covers more of the window underneath. The meter bars shrink to 5 × 10.
