REDESIGN 02 sanbluedot

What changed from 01: the capsule is small, with fully round ends, beside the duck’s head, laid out like the reference: status and target on top, the meter, then stage and engine. The wordmark left — the duck wears SBdot on its chest. Everything that is not live information left with it.

It only grows when there is something to say. Key down shows a tiny capsule and nothing else. The duck and the bubble's thought trail appear only once a voice is heard; release without speaking and the capsule simply leaves. On delivery it shows the result, then everything goes.

Three sizes, as the Dynamic Island does it: minimal 36 × 16 while it waits for a voice, full 120 × 30 while you speak, while it works and for the result, short 120 × 22 for an error, a cancel or a mode change. Content keeps an even margin from the round ends.

References used: Apple Human Interface Guidelines, Live Activities — compact, minimal and expanded presentations; consistent, concentric margins; height that changes with the information.
