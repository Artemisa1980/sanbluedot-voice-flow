station palette — proposals 01

Six rooms for the voice-station: four partners for the mint canvas, and two that change the canvas itself so keeping mint stays a measured choice rather than an assumption. Every value comes from color-palette.md or from brand canon — nothing here is invented. The window is identical in all six, so only the palette varies.

Measured before drawing. Contrast computed against the real hex values:
  • Mint is a ground, not an accent. A mint chip reaches 1.37 on greige and 1.30 on amber-light, but 8.04 on olive and 9.66 on navy. Keeping it as the canvas is right; using it as a detail on a light surface is not.
  • No status colour survives as ink on mint — sage-deep 3.26 · clay-deep 3.99 · amber-deep 1.68 · coral 1.54 · dot 1.35 · gold 1.02, all under 4.5. That is why today's small green dot reads as decoration. Status is drawn as a filled pill with a navy hairline, or inside the dark console.
  • Ink follows the fill. Dark fills take paper ink (sage-deep 5.00); bright fills take navy (amber-deep 5.77 · coral 6.28 · dot 7.17 · gold 9.43).
  • Navy passes on every candidate ground, clay 4.51 through paper 14.83, so the ink never changes with the partner.
  • The selected state stops borrowing from macOS. The ring is the dot #7CB3E8, not the system focus colour.
