MICRODUCK.SYS

PROPOSAL 01 sanbluedot

What this is: the mascot alone, before the HUD is redesigned around it. Three ways to draw the same Microduck, all built from the reference robot: dome head, camera eye with a gold ring, an orange bill that opens, a servo neck, a cream body and orange feet.

How it behaves: it stands outside the capsule. It pops up when the key goes down, faces you at rest, turns its head toward the capsule while recording, waits while processing, nods yes on delivery and shakes no on an error or a cancel.

How it moves: one articulated body — hips, knees, ankles, a three-link neck and the head — so it can stand, step, crouch, sit and look up. While you dictate, the pose follows your voice (section 3).

Every colour is an approved token except the two marked NEW. Use the deck below to drive every duck at once; LISTEN simulates a voice, and the bill follows it.

1 · THREE WAYS TO DRAW IT

A · NEWTONSTANDING · VECTOR

Closest to the photos. Standing pose, facing you, the camera eye in the middle of the face. Smooth shapes, soft shading on the dome.

Reads well at full size; the servo bolts and the eye slot start to blur below about 70pt.

B · PIXELSTANDING · SPRITE

Retro dev-station. The same duck as a 22-pixel-wide sprite. Motion moves in steps, like an old game character.

Matches Press Start 2P and the VU bars; stays sharp at any size because every pixel is a block.

C · SITTERSITTING · VECTOR

The compact pose from the sitting photo: bigger head, knees folded in front, the back panel resting on the floor.

Shortest of the three, so it fits the capsule's 88pt height without towering over it.

2 · ONE BODY, MANY POSES

READY

Facing you. The resting pose, and where it returns after every dictation.

STAND

Upright, head turned to the capsule the moment the key goes down.

LEAN

The neck bends toward the capsule: the normal listening pose.

STRETCH

Neck tall, bill wide open: your voice got louder.

LOOK UP

Neck curved forward, head up and cocked: you paused.

STEP

One leg forward, like the grey duck in the photo.

CROUCH

Knees bent, ready to spring back up.

SIT

Knees folded, neck in an S: a long dictation, it settles in to listen.

4 · BEHAVIOUR SHEET · OPTION A

ENTER

Key down. Pops up beside the capsule with a small overshoot.

LISTEN

Recording. Turns its head toward the capsule, body breathing, bill moves with your voice, coral LED.

WAIT

Processing. Looks up, then aside; the eye scans; dot-blue LED.

YES

Pasted, copied or action done. Two nods and a hop; sage LED.

NO

Error or cancelled. Shakes the head and slumps a little; red LED.
